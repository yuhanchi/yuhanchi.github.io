## Publications

**Calibrating What Behavior Can Identify: Conformal Reward Sets for Inverse Reinforcement Learning**<br>
**Yuhan Chi**. *NeurIPS 2026 Workshop on Physical Understanding for Decision-Making (PUDM)*, Poster.<br>
[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=GZXE6lszVW) · [Code](https://github.com/Chi-Shan0707/cp-irl) · [Project](https://chi-shan0707.github.io/cp-irl/)
{: .cv-entry}

- Adapts conformal inverse optimization to IRL with a coordinate-invariant, policy-value-based score for calibrated reward sets.
- Characterizes when behavioral compatibility implies true-reward coverage, and proves when robust planning over the set cannot certify improvement over a reference policy.

**Token-Level Verification under Controlled Evaluation**<br>
**Yuhan Chi**. *ICML 2026 Workshop on AI for Math (AI4Math)*, Poster.<br>
[Paper](https://openreview.net/forum?id=wRImV3kfR1) · [Code](https://github.com/Chi-Shan0707/token-verification-mirage)
{: .cv-entry}

- Shows that evaluation-protocol choices alone shift apparent verification AUROC of LLM math traces by up to **0.18**.
- Under controlled scoring, shallow token statistics reach only **0.60–0.75** AUROC; final-token entropy drops from 0.72–0.75 to chance (0.47–0.48).
