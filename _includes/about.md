I am, first of all, a mathematics student. I like *distilling messy engineering problems into clean mathematical models*, and I keep coming back to one question: **how should we make decisions under uncertainty?** That leads me to decision-making by AI systems and robots — when those decisions can be explained, and when they deserve our trust.

I also care about social and economic questions, and hope to leave the world a little better than I found it.
