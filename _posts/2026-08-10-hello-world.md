This site is my notebook in public. I plan to write short, careful notes about the things I am learning — mostly mathematics, optimization, and reinforcement learning — and occasionally longer write-ups of projects.

A few rules I want to keep:

- **Small and finished beats big and abandoned.** A one-page note that is correct is more useful than a long draft.
- **Explain the motivation first.** Definitions make more sense once you know what problem they solve.
- **Say what doesn't work.** Negative results and failed attempts are part of the story.

If something here is wrong or unclear, please tell me — the email is on the [about page](../../about/).
